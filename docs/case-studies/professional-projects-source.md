This document is the internal editorial source for the ChargeIT, GPay + Wallet, and Jio Edge Cloud Services portfolio pages. It records the claims extracted from the public Craft documents so future edits do not need to reinterpret the source material.

- Label speculative or unshipped work as a concept.

## Jio ChargeIT

Source: https://s.craft.me/AkY6amqXtg05Ry

### Verified claims

- Jio had an API-based subscription engine but needed a graphical management interface.
- A basic form covered plan creation and editing; other work was being handled from the backend.
- Sai was the only designer on the product.
- Plan creation was the first major redesign target.
- The first redesign grouped the long form into Basic, Advanced, and Pricing steps.
- Pricing showed multiple frequencies and currencies together, used flags for currency recognition, and supported contextual editing.
- Secondary research and internal sales feedback expanded the model beyond currencies and frequencies.
- Pricing models considered: freemium, flat rate, pay per quantity, pay-as-you-use, per-unit fixed price, volume-based, tiered, fixed price plus consumption, perpetual licence, and pay per active user.
- The design conclusions were to begin with prepaid versus postpaid, support a custom usage unit, keep the operator in one interface, and rebalance a centre-heavy layout.
- The pricing-table work explored editing tiers, ranges, frequencies, and currencies together.
- An internal sales colleague reported spending close to two weeks researching and reviewing pricing approaches.
- Compass was a generative pricing-assistance suggestion, not a verified shipped feature.
- Sai also worked on the product logo and illustrations.
- JioDesign Central created the interface icons.
- The business team initiated the product; product storylines were later refined with the product team for a company-conference launch.

### Contextual anecdote

An incorrect recurring furniture-rental charge continued for three months before support resolved it. This is a personal motivation and must not be presented as formal research evidence.

### Prohibited or unsupported claims

- Measured reduction in launch time, billing errors, or support effort.
- Revenue, adoption, conversion, or customer-satisfaction impact.
- Compass deployment or measured time savings.

## GPay + Wallet

Source: https://plants-invite-tm7.craft.me/dnTORobOT60OS0

### Project status

This is an independent concept exploration. It was not commissioned by Google and is not evidence of a shipped Google product.

### Verified activities and artefacts

- The project asks how Google Wallet could become useful in India alongside established GPay and UPI behaviour.
- Exploration included desk research into UPI, low-value transactions, offline payments, document storage, account aggregation, trust, and privacy.
- Sai reviewed the Google Wallet Android experience.
- A detailed interview guide was drafted around wallet use, documents, travel, security, rewards, finance, and micro-transactions.
- The Craft source does not include a participant count, completed interview record, or synthesised participant evidence.
- Opportunity areas explored included savings pockets, budget pockets, round-ups, loyalty, document storage, ticketing, transaction grouping, budgeting, and year-end reflection.
- Savings pockets were framed as goal-oriented money; budget pockets were framed as spending allowances.
- The concept explored pockets before, during, and after payment.
- Designed flows include paying from a pocket, saving spare change after payment, choosing spare change during payment, grouping transactions, category balances, and a story-style annual review.

### Prohibited or unsupported claims

- User adoption, trust, or behavioural change validated through research.
- A completed interview study unless new records are supplied.
- Affiliation with Google or access to Google product data.
- Shipped status or commercial impact.
- Current market-size statistics without a fresh, cited source.

## Jio Edge Cloud Services

Source: https://plants-invite-tm7.craft.me/En6TP9RC4KyXrE

### Verified project facts

- Client/product: Jio Edge Cloud Services (JECS).
- Role: Design Owner.
- Responsibilities listed in the source: UX Design, UX Flows, Research, Marketing, Branding.
- Industries: Cloud Services and 5G.
- Start: January 2024.
- Timeline: four months.
- Documented workstreams: Identity & Access Management, product video, branding and marketing, and operations-dashboard data visualisation.
- Sai created extensive journey maps for users, roles, user groups, and policies.
- Orange items in the journey map represented work that was pending when the map was created.
- The IAM material includes a pipeline interaction for creating roles and permissions.
- The product video was created in Keynote.
- Logo animation was created using Jitter and Figma.

- One-month delivery timeline.
- A measured reduction from five-to-seven steps to one-to-two steps.
- Measured error reduction, task-time improvement, confidence, or trust.

- Specific competitive-research conclusions not present in the source document.
